# MVP Scope

## In scope

- Tenants, organizations, users, scoped RBAC and MFA.
- Framework/version/requirement engine and custom framework import.
- Framework crosswalk.
- Common controls and control implementations.
- Assets and processes.
- Risk register, configurable methodology, historical risk evaluations and treatments.
- Compliance assessments, evidence, findings and CAPA/actions.
- Internal audit/workpapers and control tests.
- Documents, versions, approvals and report templates.
- RTP, SoA, audit/compliance/management reports.
- Workflow and notifications.
- AI provider abstraction, RAG and human-reviewed assistants.
- REST/OpenAPI, Docker/on-premise deployment and air-gapped path.

## Explicitly post-MVP

- Full TPRM/vendor portal.
- Full BCM/BIA suite.
- Full incident-management suite.
- Built-in vulnerability scanner/SIEM/SOAR.
- Autonomous AI agents.
- Native mobile apps.
- Graph database as system of record.
- Quantitative risk engines such as FAIR/Monte Carlo beyond extension hooks.
