# Product Vision

Build a Persian/English, sovereign, framework-agnostic GRC operating platform capable of supporting Iranian and international governance, risk, security, quality and digital-transformation frameworks without rewriting the application for each standard.

The product is not intended to be a static checklist. Its core promise is to connect organizational objectives, assets/processes, risks, controls, requirements, evidence, testing, assessments, audits, findings, remediation and management reporting in one traceable system.

Key differentiators:

1. **Implement once, comply many** through a common-control model and framework crosswalks.
2. **Document factory** for outputs such as RTP, SoA, audit and executive reports.
3. **Sovereign AI** with local/air-gapped providers and human approval.
4. **Persian-first enterprise UX** alongside full English support.
5. **On-premise first-class support** for sensitive organizations.
6. **Evidence-driven integrations** that collect facts from infrastructure without autonomously declaring compliance.
