# Content Licensing

Framework text and implementation guidance may be subject to copyright or licensing restrictions.

## Content classes

- `PUBLIC`: material that can legally be redistributed with the product.
- `LICENSED`: material that may be embedded only under a product/distribution license.
- `CUSTOMER_PROVIDED`: content imported by a customer under that customer's own rights.
- `INTERNAL`: organization-specific policies, controls, maturity models and checklists.

## Rules

- Do not commit proprietary ISO or COBIT text without explicit redistribution rights.
- Do not assume public accessibility equals redistribution permission.
- Keep framework metadata/licensing provenance with every content pack.
- Prefer original common-control descriptions and mappings where legally appropriate.
- Customer-provided framework content must remain tenant-scoped and must not silently become global product content.
- AI/RAG ingestion must honor the same content/license scope as normal application access.
